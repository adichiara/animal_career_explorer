## How to read the figures

- **Direct costs:** annual tuition, required fees, and the stated housing/meal arrangement. Extra program/course fees are included only where explicitly noted.
- **Full cost of attendance (COA):** the school's direct-cost budget plus allowances for books, travel and personal expenses. This is an aid budget, not necessarily the bill.
- **Net direct-cost scenario:** direct costs minus the specified grants/scholarships. It is not a personalized aid prediction.
- **Loans and work-study:** financing and earnings, respectively; do not subtract them when comparing the underlying price.
- Prices generally cover fall and spring, not a full 12 months. Summer coursework, internships, housing, travel, insurance, equipment and credential/exam costs may add to the total.
- A school may publish estimates rather than final rates. Preserve that qualification.
- An unknown aid amount is **TBD**, not zero. Do not count unawarded aid as a dependable discount.

## Fall 2028 projection method

These are **scenarios, not published 2028 prices or award guarantees**.

Let D be 2026–27 direct costs, A the assumed annual renewable award, and g the annual cost increase. With g = 3% and A held fixed:

- First-year 2028–29 net direct cost = D × (1 + g)^2 − A.
- Four-year 2028–32 net direct total = D × [(1 + g)^2 + (1 + g)^3 + (1 + g)^4 + (1 + g)^5] − 4A.
- Recalculate separately by year if housing, campus, degree status, fees or awards change. Deduct a one-time award only once.
- Test 2% and 4% cost growth as sensitivities; the 3% case is a modeling choice.

| Example | D | A | 2028 first-year net direct | 2028–32 four-year net direct |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| UMass residential baseline, no aid assumed | $37,256 | $0 | approximately $39,525 | approximately $165,357 |
| UNE with the conditional $28,000 merit award | $67,440 | $28,000 | approximately $43,547 | approximately $187,326 |

Both examples exclude indirect expenses and unmodeled additional charges. UMass campus/housing changes and UNE room selection require a more detailed year-by-year budget.

Do not carry forward earlier four-year totals that merely inflate an already-discounted net price: fixed-dollar scholarships do not automatically rise with tuition. Do not treat past 529 projections as current balances.
