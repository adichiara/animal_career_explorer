Animal & Science Path Explorer — updated prototype

Open index.html in a browser. No server, login, or internet connection is required for the core site.
Responses, saved careers, saved college paths, progression inputs, and snapshots are stored in the browser's local storage.

This version adds:
- Evidence-backed exploration across 93 canonical roles, 32 role families, observed employer titles, current/recent postings, qualification standards, credentials, and explicit progression links.
- A Market & Evidence view that distinguishes canonical roles from employer titles and shows transparent source and coverage information.
- Support for reviewed AZA complete-board observations; unreviewed automated match suggestions are never published as career evidence.
- Career Progression: explore natural growth roles, possible growth roles, and lateral options from a current or hypothetical career using assumed education and experience.
- Career Explorer filters for saved careers and careers supported by saved college paths.
- College Path Explorer filters for saved paths and paths supporting saved careers.
- Broader college-path feature filters that cut across multiple programs, with Any/All matching.

Companion research files are in the resources folder.
