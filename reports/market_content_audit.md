## Formal qualification and credential evidence

### Conservation geneticist / wildlife genomicist — Federal Genetics Series 0440
- Organization: U.S. Office of Personnel Management
- Education: Degree in genetics or a basic biological science.
- Coursework: At least 9 semester hours in genetics for related biological-science degrees.
- Experience: Graduate training is common because specialized genetics preparation may be limited at undergraduate level.
- Source: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/classification-qualifications/general-schedule-qualification-standards/0400/genetics-series-0440/

### Research ecologist — Federal Ecology Series 0408
- Organization: U.S. Office of Personnel Management
- Education: Degree in biology or related field underlying ecological research.
- Coursework: At least 30 semester hours biological sciences, including 9 ecology and 12 physical/mathematical sciences.
- Experience: Grade-specific experience/graduate education applies.
- Source: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/classification-qualifications/general-schedule-qualification-standards/0400/ecology-series-0408/

### Wildlife biologist — Federal Wildlife Biology Series 0486
- Organization: U.S. Office of Personnel Management
- Education: Degree or equivalent biological-science preparation.
- Coursework: Nonresearch positions: at least 9 semester hours wildlife subjects, 12 zoology, and 9 botany/plant sciences. Research positions also require 15 hours across physical/mathematical/earth sciences.
- Experience: Grade-specific experience/education applies beyond the basic requirement.
- Source: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/classification-qualifications/general-schedule-qualification-standards/0400/wildlife-biology-series-0486/

### Wildlife refuge manager — Federal Wildlife Refuge Management Series 0485
- Organization: U.S. Office of Personnel Management
- Education: Degree in zoology, wildlife management, or appropriate biology field, or equivalent combination.
- Coursework: At least 9 semester hours zoology; 6 wildlife courses; 3 botany; 3 conservation biology.
- Experience: Progressive grade-specific experience applies for manager positions.
- Source: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/classification-qualifications/general-schedule-qualification-standards/0400/wildlife-refuge-management-series-0485/

### Zoologist — Federal Zoology Series 0410
- Organization: U.S. Office of Personnel Management
- Education: Degree in zoology or related science.
- Coursework: At least 20 semester hours in zoology and related animal sciences for related-discipline degrees.
- Experience: Grade-specific experience/graduate education applies.
- Source: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/classification-qualifications/general-schedule-qualification-standards/0400/zoology-series-0410/
