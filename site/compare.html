How to read this table

Animal experience names the actual fieldwork, clinical practice, research or internship structure. Observation, animal handling and day-to-day husbandry provide different kinds of preparation.

Study examples show specific courses or subject areas. Requirements and electives are identified where established; these are examples, not a complete curriculum.

Planning considerations highlight differences that can affect the choice: species studied, campus moves, elective planning, and how a minor, certificate or associate-to-bachelor sequence fits together. Sources are linked in each card.
