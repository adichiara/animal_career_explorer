Animal Pathways reference site

The public homepage contains three sections: Field Guides, College Programs, and Job Examples. The pages use a shared article-style layout and source-backed content.

Because Field Guide content is loaded from JSON, serve the `site` directory through a local web server when previewing edits. College and job content is drawn from `data.js`.

The prior full research interface is preserved at `legacy.html`, and the prior preference explorer is preserved at `discover.html`. They are intentionally not linked from the public homepage or primary navigation.

Companion research files are in the resources folder.
