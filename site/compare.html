Animal experience varies. Field research, observation, clinical practice and direct animal care are different kinds of experience. A hands-on tag alone does not establish a requirement. Where the record does not establish required animal contact, the table says so.

Skills listed are the stronger preparation areas described in each program guide. They summarize the existing curriculum review; they are not standardized ratings, guarantees, or rankings. Electives, concentrations, minors and certificates can determine what a student actually studies.
